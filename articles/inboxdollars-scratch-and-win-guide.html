How to think about small bonus features without confusing them for a full strategy.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20265 min read

Quick Answer

Scratch and Win style bonuses should be treated as extra upside, not your main earning method. Focus first on offers and tasks with clear requirements and trackable rewards.

Bonus Feature Mindset

Small gamified rewards are fun, but beginners should not build an entire plan around uncertain bonuses.

Better Focus

Use your time on tasks where the requirement and reward are clear, then treat bonus features as a little extra.
