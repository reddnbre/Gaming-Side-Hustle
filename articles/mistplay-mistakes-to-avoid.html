Mistplay Mistakes To Avoid

Avoid the simple mistakes that can cost beginners time, tracking, or reward progress.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20267 min read

Quick Answer

The biggest Mistplay mistakes are installing games outside Mistplay, not launching games through the app, ignoring time and level caps, chasing games you dislike, and not checking reward status carefully.

Mistake 1: Installing Outside Mistplay

Mistplay says games should be installed through Mistplay to be eligible for rewards. This is the first thing beginners should protect.

Mistake 2: Not Launching Through The App

Launch games through Mistplay so playtime can count correctly. Make this a habit before every session.

Mistake 3: Ignoring Caps

Mistplay notes a two-hour-per-day GXP cap per game and a game level cap of 20. Track when it is time to switch.

Mistake 4: Chasing Every Boost

Boosted games can help, but only if they fit your play style. Do not force a game you hate just because it is boosted.

Start Clean

You can start Mistplay here and remember to install games through the app.
