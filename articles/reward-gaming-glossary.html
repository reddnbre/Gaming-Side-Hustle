Reward Gaming Glossary

The simple terms beginners need before starting game offers or rewards apps.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20266 min read

Quick Answer

Reward gaming terms are easier once you know the basics: offers have requirements, milestones define goals, tracking records proof, and payouts may pend before becoming available.

Key Terms

  • Offer wall: a list of reward tasks.
  • Milestone: the required goal.
  • Pending: reward is waiting to finalize.
  • Tracking: recording proof and progress.
  • Cashout: redeeming rewards.

Why This Matters

Knowing the vocabulary helps you read terms and avoid bad offers.
