How To Earn Units On Mistplay

Understand units, GXP, checkpoints, boosted games, and daily earning limits before you play.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20267 min read

Quick Answer

You earn Mistplay units by playing eligible games installed and launched through Mistplay, collecting GXP, reaching checkpoints, and sometimes using boosted games, events, or eligible referral bonuses.

What Are Units?

Units are Mistplay's reward currency that can be redeemed in the Shop for available rewards such as gift cards or sweepstakes entries.

GXP And Checkpoints

Mistplay explains that users earn GXP as they play. When enough GXP is collected to reach checkpoints, users can earn units.

Boosted Games

Mistplay says boosted games can temporarily multiply reward speed. These can be useful, but only if the game is something you will actually play.

Know The Limits

Mistplay's support article notes a two-hour-per-day GXP cap per game and a game level cap of 20. That means switching games can matter after you hit a cap.

FAQ

Can one game earn forever?

Mistplay notes a game level cap of 20, so you may need to switch games after reaching that point.

Do boosted games matter?

They can help, but only when the game fits your time and interests.
