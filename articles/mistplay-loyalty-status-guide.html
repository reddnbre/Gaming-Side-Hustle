Mistplay Loyalty Status Guide

How loyalty status can affect GXP, gems, multipliers, and Mistplay benefits.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20266 min read

Quick Answer

Mistplay Loyalty Status is an in-app progress system with tiers such as Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum. Higher status can unlock benefits like extra gems, increased GXP, higher unit multipliers in select games, bonus units for sweepstakes, and avatar frame perks.

What Loyalty Status Is

Mistplay says Loyalty Status is based on in-app progress tied to gems over a biweekly period. Users can move through tiers and monitor status directly in the app.

Possible Benefits

Mistplay's support article lists benefits such as extra gems, increased GXP earnings, higher maximum unit multipliers in select games, bonus units for weekly sweepstakes, and premium avatar frames.

Beginner Strategy

Do not chase status blindly. Treat status as a bonus system. Your main focus should still be playing eligible games through Mistplay and choosing games you can progress in.
