Best Games On Mistplay For Beginners

The best Mistplay game is the one you can play consistently through the app without burning out.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20267 min read

Quick Answer

The best Mistplay games for beginners are games you enjoy, games installed directly through Mistplay, boosted games when available, and games where you can reach checkpoints without forcing long sessions.

There Is No Single Best Game

Mistplay recommendations can vary by region, device, profile, and availability. Instead of chasing one universal best game, learn how to compare the games you see in your own app.

Use Top Pick And Boosted Games

Mistplay's beginner guide describes Top Pick recommendations and boosted games. Top Pick can suggest a game based on what you already play, while boosted games may temporarily earn faster.

Think In Checkpoints

Since units are tied to GXP checkpoints, choose games you can actually progress in. A game with great potential is not useful if you stop after ten minutes.

Respect Time Caps

Mistplay notes a two-hour-per-day GXP cap per game. After that, switching games may be smarter than grinding the same title.

FAQ

Are boosted games always best?

Not always. They help most when you actually enjoy the game and can reach checkpoints.

Should I install games outside Mistplay?

No. Mistplay says games should be installed and launched through Mistplay to be eligible for rewards.
