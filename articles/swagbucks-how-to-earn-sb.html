How To Earn SB On Swagbucks

The main earning paths and how to choose the ones that fit your routine.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20266 min read

Quick Answer

You can earn SB on Swagbucks through eligible activities such as playing games, answering surveys, shopping, searching the web, scanning receipts, and discovering deals.

Main Earning Methods

Swagbucks lists several ways to earn: games, surveys, shopping, search, receipts, videos, and deals. Beginners should test one method at a time.

Best Beginner Method

Game offers can be interesting if you like mobile games. Surveys and receipts are simpler, but may be smaller. Shopping only makes sense when you were already going to buy.

Track Your SB

Record what you did, when you started, the expected SB, and when it credits. This helps you learn which tasks are worth repeating.
