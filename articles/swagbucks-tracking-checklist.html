Swagbucks Tracking Checklist

Track every game offer before the details blur together.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20265 min read

Quick Answer

Track the offer name, game, platform, SB amount, install date, deadline, required milestone, screenshots, spending, pending date, and final credit status.

Fields To Track

  • Game title
  • SB reward
  • Deadline
  • Milestone
  • Screenshots
  • Pending or credited status

Why It Matters

Tracking prevents confusion and helps you learn which offers are worth repeating.
