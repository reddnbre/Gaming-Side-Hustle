RollerCoin Mistakes To Avoid

Most beginner mistakes come from spending resources before understanding the system.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20267 min read

Quick Answer

The biggest RollerCoin beginner mistakes are overspending RLT, buying miners without checking rack fit, wasting parts, merging too early, chasing every event, and ignoring marketplace prices.

Mistake 1: Overspending RLT

RLT is used across the Marketplace and Forge ecosystem. Spending it all at once can block better opportunities later.

Mistake 2: Buying Random Miners

A miner needs to fit your room plan. Check power, bonus, cells, set potential, and marketplace price before buying.

Mistake 3: Wasting Parts

RollerCoin parts are useful for crafting and merging. Selling or spending them without checking recipes can hurt future upgrades.

Mistake 4: Merging Too Early

Merge can be powerful, but it consumes miners, parts, and RLT. Use the merge checklist before upgrading.

Mistake 5: Chasing Every Event

Events can be valuable, but not every event fits every account. Choose the ones that match your resources and time.

Mistake 6: Not Tracking Decisions

Keep notes on purchases, merges, parts, and event goals. A simple tracker prevents repeated mistakes.

FAQ

What is the biggest RollerCoin beginner mistake?

Spending resources before checking whether the move supports your room, Forge, marketplace, or merge plan.

Should beginners merge right away?

Only if the upgrade is clearly worth the miners, parts, and RLT used.
