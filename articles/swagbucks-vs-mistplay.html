Swagbucks vs Mistplay

Which reward gaming starting point fits you better?

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20267 min read

Quick Answer

Mistplay is simpler for mobile gaming rewards, while Swagbucks offers more earning types, including games, surveys, shopping, receipts, and search.

Mistplay

Mistplay is focused on mobile games, GXP, units, checkpoints, and reward redemption.

Swagbucks

Swagbucks is broader and includes games, surveys, shopping, receipts, search, and more.

Which Should Beginners Pick?

Pick Mistplay if you want a simpler game-first app. Pick Swagbucks if you want multiple ways to earn.
