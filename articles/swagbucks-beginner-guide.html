Swagbucks Beginner Guide

A simple starting point for earning SB through games, surveys, shopping, receipts, and other everyday activities.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20267 min read

Quick Answer

Swagbucks is a rewards platform where members earn points called SB for eligible activities like games, surveys, shopping, searching, and receipts, then redeem SB for gift cards or PayPal cash.

What Is Swagbucks?

Swagbucks describes itself as a free rewards service where members earn SB for online and mobile activities. Those SB can be redeemed for gift cards or cash rewards.

How To Start

Start with one or two earning methods. If you want gaming rewards, choose games through Swagbucks and read all offer terms before installing anything.

Beginner Plan

  • Join and verify your account.
  • Pick one earning method.
  • Track terms and deadlines.
  • Redeem small rewards while learning.
