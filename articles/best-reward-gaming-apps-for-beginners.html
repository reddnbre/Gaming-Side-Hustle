Best Reward Gaming Apps For Beginners

A simple way to choose where to start without signing up for everything at once.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20267 min read

Quick Answer

Beginners should start with one simple app or platform, such as Mistplay for casual mobile gaming, Swagbucks for broad reward options, InboxDollars for cash-oriented tasks, or RollerCoin for a game-like mining simulator.

Mistplay

Mistplay is simple for mobile gamers who want app-based rewards.

Swagbucks

Swagbucks offers games, surveys, shopping, receipts, search, and rewards.

InboxDollars

InboxDollars is cash-focused and includes games, surveys, emails, and offers.

RollerCoin

RollerCoin is more game-like and has deeper systems such as miners, power, rooms, Forge, and Marketplace.

Where To Start

Pick one platform, track your first week, and expand only after you understand the basics.
