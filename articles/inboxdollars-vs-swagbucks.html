InboxDollars vs Swagbucks

Which rewards platform makes more sense for beginners?

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20267 min read

Quick Answer

Swagbucks uses SB points and offers many earning methods, while InboxDollars is more cash-oriented. Beginners can try either, but should track offers carefully on both.

Swagbucks

Swagbucks is broad: games, surveys, shopping, receipts, search, and more.

InboxDollars

InboxDollars is cash-oriented and includes games, offers, surveys, emails, and related reward tasks.

Best Choice

Try Swagbucks if you like points and many earning methods. Try InboxDollars if you prefer a cash-focused platform.
