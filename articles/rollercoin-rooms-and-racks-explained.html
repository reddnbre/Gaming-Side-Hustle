RollerCoin Rooms And Racks Explained

Your miners only matter when your room and rack setup can actually use them well.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20266 min read

Quick Answer

Rooms and racks control how many miners you can place and how efficiently your setup works. Beginners should plan rack space before buying miners, watch set bonuses, and avoid collecting miners that cannot fit into a useful room setup.

Why Racks Matter

Miners need space. Racks provide cells, and different miners may take different cell sizes. A strong miner is less useful if your room cannot place it cleanly.

Sets And Bonuses

RollerCoin's Sets FAQ explains that sets combine racks and miners for additional bonuses. A set can include specific components and reward extra power when completed and installed.

Buying Racks

The Marketplace FAQ lists racks as one of the item groups players can buy and sell for RLT. That means beginners can use marketplace filters to compare rack options instead of guessing.

Beginner Room Plan

  • Know your available cells before buying miners.
  • Keep miners that fit current or near-future racks.
  • Check whether a rack helps complete a set.
  • Do not buy a miner only to leave it in storage.

Sources Used

FAQ

Do racks affect strategy?

Yes. Rack space determines how easily you can place miners and complete useful setups.

What are sets?

Sets combine specific racks and miners for bonus power when completed and installed.
