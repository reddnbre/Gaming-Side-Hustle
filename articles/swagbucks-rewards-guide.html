Swagbucks Rewards Guide

Understand SB value, gift cards, PayPal cash, and smart redemption habits.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20266 min read

Quick Answer

Swagbucks rewards are redeemed with SB points. Swagbucks says 100 SB is generally equal to $1 in reward value, with rewards including gift cards and PayPal cash.

SB Value

Swagbucks states that rewards are earned in points called SB and that 100 SB equals $1 USD in rewards.

Reward Options

Reward options can include gift cards from popular brands and PayPal cash. Availability and minimum redemption amounts may vary.

Redeem Small While Learning

Beginners should consider redeeming smaller rewards first. This helps you understand the process and build confidence before stacking larger balances.
