Mistplay Tracking Checklist

A simple way to track your games, units, checkpoints, and reward redemptions.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20265 min read

Quick Answer

Track Mistplay by writing down the game name, install date, whether it was installed through Mistplay, GXP progress, checkpoints reached, units earned, time played, boosted status, and reward redemption status.

Why Track Mistplay?

Mistplay is simpler than many game offer walls, but tracking still helps you spot which games are worth your time and when you should switch.

Fields To Track

  • Game name
  • Install date
  • Installed through Mistplay
  • Boosted or normal
  • Session time
  • Checkpoints reached
  • Units earned
  • Reward redemption status

Track Caps

Mistplay notes a two-hour-per-day GXP cap per game and a game level cap of 20. Tracking helps you stop grinding one game after it stops being efficient.

Check Mistplay

You can open Mistplay here and start tracking from your first game.

FAQ

Do I need a spreadsheet?

No. A notes app is enough when you are starting.

What matters most?

Track whether the game was installed through Mistplay, your time, checkpoints, units, and reward status.
