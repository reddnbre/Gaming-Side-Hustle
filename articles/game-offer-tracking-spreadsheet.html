Game Offer Tracking Spreadsheet

The columns that keep reward gaming organized.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20265 min read

Quick Answer

Your game offer spreadsheet should track platform, game, start date, deadline, milestone, reward, spending, screenshots, pending date, and payout status.

Best Columns

  • Platform
  • Game
  • Reward
  • Deadline
  • Milestone
  • Proof
  • Status

Keep It Simple

A simple tracker you actually use is better than a complicated one you ignore.
