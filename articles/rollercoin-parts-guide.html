RollerCoin Parts Guide For Beginners

Parts are more than inventory clutter. They are crafting and merging resources that can change your account path.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20266 min read

Quick Answer

RollerCoin parts are used for crafting and merging. Beginners should save useful parts, compare marketplace prices, and avoid spending rare parts unless the recipe clearly improves mining power, merge progress, or long-term value.

What Parts Do

RollerCoin's FAQ explains that parts help improve miner effectiveness through crafting and merging, making stronger miners possible at a reduced cost compared with buying everything directly.

Part Rarity

RollerCoin lists multiple rarity tiers for parts. Rarity matters because recipes and merges may require specific types or levels, and rarer parts may carry more marketplace value.

How To Get Parts

The FAQ notes that players can earn parts from mini-game rewards, obtain common parts cases from the Season Store, or buy needed parts from the Marketplace.

Beginner Parts Strategy

  • Do not sell every leftover part immediately.
  • Check merge and Forge recipes first.
  • Compare marketplace prices before buying.
  • Save rare parts until you have a clear plan.

FAQ

What are parts used for?

They are used in crafting and merging to help create or upgrade miners.

Can you buy parts?

Yes. RollerCoin says parts can be bought from the Marketplace.
