RollerCoin Forge Guide For Beginners

The Forge is where RollerCoin crafting, merging, and long-term resource decisions start to come together.

By Gaming Side Hustle BlueprintAugust 3, 20268 min read

Quick Answer

The RollerCoin Forge lets players craft items, merge miners and parts, view recipe requirements, gain Forge experience, unlock discounts, and eventually use bulk merge. Beginners should use it slowly and prioritize recipes that improve their room or preserve value.

What Is The Forge?

RollerCoin describes the Forge as a dedicated device in the Service Room. It supports crafting and merging, and every player receives a Forge for free. It is not just a menu; it is a progression system tied to recipes and resources.

What Can You Do In The Forge?

Inside the Forge, you can craft assets like miners, parts, racks, lootboxes, and batteries, while also merging miners and parts into stronger versions. Recipes show required components, RLT, duration, and available resources.

Why Forge Levels Matter

Crafting and merging can give Forge experience. Higher levels unlock new recipes, better efficiency, visual upgrades, and discounts. RollerCoin notes that merge discounts can reduce RLT cost and component cost, while higher levels can also unlock bulk merge.

Bulk Merge Strategy

Bulk merge for parts unlocks before bulk merge for miners. This can save time, but it can also make it easier to spend resources too quickly. Use bulk merge after you know exactly what you want to upgrade.

Beginner Forge Plan

  • Review recipes before spending resources.
  • Prioritize upgrades that improve your active room.
  • Keep some RLT and parts in reserve.
  • Watch limited-time recipe dates.
  • Do not craft just because a recipe is available.

FAQ

Is the Forge free?

RollerCoin says every player receives one Forge placed in the Service Room.

Can crafting and merging happen together?

RollerCoin says crafting and merging do not block each other, though only one active craft runs at a time.
